## Key takeaways

- The Thursday returns desk closes at 19:00. Booked workshop sessions can run until 20:00. · [[#Source evidence 1|Evidence]]

## What it means · Agent interpretation

Put the desk and workshop hours on separate lines so borrowers can tell whether staff will be available for their return.

## Source text

### Winter schedule

Starting Thursday, November 26, the returns desk closes at 19:00. The workshop room stays open until 20:00 for booked sessions.

### Returning tools after 19:00

The side-door drop box remains available after the returns desk closes. Tools with rechargeable batteries must be returned to a staff member during desk hours.
