# What changes at the Thursday returns desk

## What changed

From November 26, the Thursday returns desk closes at 19:00 while booked workshop sessions can continue until 20:00. [[10_sources/Winter desk-hours notice#Winter schedule|Read the notice]]

## What to do

Use the side-door drop box for eligible tools after 19:00. Plan to bring rechargeable tools to a staff member during desk hours. [[10_sources/Winter desk-hours notice#Returning tools after 19:00|Check the return instructions]]

## What we can learn from the trial

Three one-hour extensions produced three completed returns. The log records completed returns and leaves other visits uncounted. [[10_sources/Thursday desk trial log#Return tally|Check the tally]]
